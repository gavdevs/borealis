# Borealis

Choose what reaches your phone.

Borealis brings essential Android apps to Light Phone III without putting a full
app store in your pocket. Search on the companion website, add an eligible app to
your personal library, then install it on your phone.

## What it does

- A personal library shared across your paired phones.
- Install and update actions on the phone, with installed and update-available states.
- Real download bars showing percentage, transferred megabytes, and file progress.
- Clear verification, installation, and Android-confirmation states.
- Periodic update checks for installed library apps.
- A monochrome interface built with the Light Phone SDK.

## Getting started

1. Install Borealis and open [borealis.loosewire.dev](https://borealis.loosewire.dev)
   on a computer or another device.
2. Create a Borealis username and password. No email address is required.
3. Pair your Light Phone using the code shown in Borealis.
4. Sign in with Google inside Borealis on the phone and allow Android installs.
5. Search on the website, add an app to your library, then refresh Borealis and
   choose Install. Complete Android's confirmation if requested.

Your Google Play connection is saved on the phone, not sent to the companion.
Once connected, the home button says Signed in and opens connection management.
The Borealis website account and Google account are separate.

## Important limits

Borealis is experimental, independently developed, and sideload-only. It is not
an official Light tool or affiliated with Light, Aurora OSS, or Google.

The companion filters off-purpose apps such as games, social networks, email,
browsers, and entertainment using automatic metadata-based rules. This filter is
imperfect and only affects installation through Borealis; it is not phone-wide
blocking or parental control. There is no curator-approval step.

Google Play access uses an unofficial integration and can change or stop working.
An app being available or installable does not guarantee that it works on Light
Phone. Updates can require Android confirmation. Borealis needs its hosted
companion and an internet connection for discovery and delivery.

Web screenshots use fictional demo app names. Phone screenshots are real LP3
captures. Neither is a promise that a specific banking or other app will work.
