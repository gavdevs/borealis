# Borealis 0.1.6

A few essential apps, without a full app store on your Light Phone.

**[Sign in or create an account → borealis.loosewire.dev](https://borealis.loosewire.dev)**

[Pairing and setup guide](https://github.com/gavdevs/borealis#readme) · [Download the APK](https://github.com/gavdevs/borealis/releases/download/v0.1.6/borealis-v0.1.6-vc11.apk)

## What's new

- Your personal library: search on the website, add eligible apps, then install them on your phone. No curator approval.
- Real download progress with percentages, megabytes, and clear installation stages.
- Installed and update-available status, with update actions and periodic checks.
- A **Signed in** button when your Google connection is saved.
- Borealis's monochrome arrow icon for the launcher.

Create a Borealis username and password, pair your phone, then connect Google Play
inside the phone app. Your Google connection stays on your phone, separate from
your website account. The setup guide walks through each step.

## Before installing

Borealis is an independent, experimental app for **Light Phone III**, not an
official Light tool. App compatibility varies. Google Play access uses an
unofficial integration that may change or stop working; Google sign-in is
required. Android may ask you to confirm installations and updates.

Install the **APK** below. The other assets provide corresponding source, SDK
patches, dependency information, license notices, and checksums. Borealis is
licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
